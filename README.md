# Totem+

Five new totems for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). Each one activates on lethal damage instead of
death, restores its own amount of health and applies its own effect.

## Totems

| Totem | Health restored | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Angel Totem | 5 hearts | Strength II + Speed II (15s), Regeneration I (20s), levitation on hostile mobs within 8 blocks |
| Devil Totem | 3 hearts | Strength II + Speed II (15s), blood harvest: 4 hearts of damage to hostile mobs within 8 blocks, heals for every victim |
| Storm Totem | 5 hearts | Speed III, Regeneration I, Resistance I, Haste I (15s), lightning strikes up to 8 hostile mobs within 15 blocks |
| Berserker Totem | 2 hearts | Strength IV, Speed III, Haste III, Resistance I (15s) |
| Guardian Totem | 6 hearts | Resistance III + Regeneration II (20s), Absorption IV (30s), removes all harmful effects, Weakness II on hostile mobs within 10 blocks |

**Totem+** expands the Minecraft totem system by adding new types - each with its own theme, effects, and crafting recipe. Each totem activates upon taking fatal damage, saving the player and granting a unique set of abilities, ranging from angelic healing to berserker rage.

#### Features

- Each has unique effects, textures, and crafting recipes

- They function just like the vanilla totem: activating automatically upon death and being consumed in the process

- Each totem restores a different amount of health

- Balanced for both single-player and multiplayer gameplay

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

![line-1](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/203fb87b3b2415d957125a2e6c603075e8f3804e.png)

#### The Totems

- **Angel Totem** - Levitates all nearby enemies, grants Strength II, Speed II, Regeneration I
  
- **Devil Totem** - Deals magic damage to enemies and heals you for each one hit (Blood Harvest)
  
- **Storm Totem** - Summons lightning bolts, sets enemies on fire, knocks them back, grants Speed III, Regeneration I, Resistance I, Haste I
  
- **Berserker Totem** - Massive Strength IV, Speed III, Haste III, Resistance I — high risk, high reward
  
- **Guardian Totem** - Resistance III, Absorption IV, Regeneration II, removes all negative effects, weakens enemies

#### Crafting

All totems are crafted at a crafting table: place a vanilla Totem of Undying in the center and surround it with thematic materials (feathers, blaze powder, copper, spider eyes, echo shards, etc.).
